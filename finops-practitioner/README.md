# FinOps Practitioner Plugin for Claude

Inventory AI spend, allocate costs to teams, compute cost-per-successful-task, and draft spend reports and renewal briefs.

Built by [The AI Career Lab](https://theaicareerlab.com/professions/finops-practitioner) — AI tools, guides, and the AI Spend Intelligence hub, designed specifically for FinOps practitioners managing AI costs.

## Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/ai-spend-inventory` | Consolidate AI subscriptions, API usage, credits, and embedded AI into one normalized spend inventory with owners, renewal dates, and flags |
| `/allocate-ai-costs` | Turn consolidated AI bills into a team-level showback or chargeback allocation with explicit rules and an unallocated remainder |
| `/cost-per-task` | Compute cost-per-successful-task unit economics from usage and cost data, including retries, failures, and quality thresholds |
| `/ai-spend-report` | Draft an executive-ready monthly AI spend report — headline, drivers, per-team view, anomalies, and decisions needed |
| `/vendor-renewal-brief` | Build a renewal negotiation brief for an AI vendor — utilization evidence, consolidation options, ask list, and walk-away position |

## Skills

This plugin includes skills that activate automatically when you're working on AI spend tasks:

- **AI Billing Models** — fluency across seat, usage, credit, reserved-capacity, hybrid, and embedded-AI billing, with normalization rules and the contract mechanics worth flagging
- **AI Unit Economics** — the cost-per-successful-task discipline: honest numerators (retries, evals, amortized subscriptions), honest denominators (quality bar), and the counterfactual

## Usage examples

```
/ai-spend-inventory
Here's what I know we pay for: Copilot Business for 120 eng seats,
ChatGPT Team for 45 marketing/ops seats, an OpenAI API key billing
~$3,800/mo, a $25k Anthropic credit purchase from March (maybe half
used?), and Notion AI came bundled in our workspace upgrade. Renewals
unknown except Copilot (November). Build the inventory and flag gaps.
```

```
/allocate-ai-costs
Attached: last month's consolidated AI bill ($41,200) and our team list
(Platform 22, Product Eng 48, Marketing 15, Support 30). The OpenAI key
is shared by Product Eng and Support; everything else maps to one team.
Propose allocation rules and give me the showback table.
```

```
/cost-per-task
Support-ticket summarization workflow: 18,400 API requests last month,
$2,950 total model cost, 11% retry rate, evals add ~8% overhead. A
"success" is a summary the agent ships without edits — that's about 82%
of completions. What does one successful summary cost, and what moves it?
```

```
/vendor-renewal-brief
ChatGPT Team renews Jan 15, 45 seats at $25/seat/mo billed annually.
Dashboard shows 29 weekly-active users. We also have Claude Team (largely
overlapping usage). Notice window 30 days. Build my negotiation brief.
```

## Disclaimer

All spend analysis, allocation, and reporting content generated by this plugin is for informational and drafting purposes only. It does not constitute accounting, tax, legal, or procurement advice. Normalized and estimated figures are working numbers for cost visibility — invoices and vendor dashboards are the source of truth, accounting treatment belongs to your controller, and contract terms need procurement or legal review before you act on them.
